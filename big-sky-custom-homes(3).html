The Big Sky Legacy

Three generations of building Arizona's finest homes.

Big Sky Custom Homes began in 1997 with a single lot in north Scottsdale and a promise: build as if your own name would be signed to the foundation. That promise has now passed through three generations of the Robert family, and hundreds of homes across the Arizona high desert.

The Big Sky Difference

01

Designed around your life.

Before a single line is drawn, we spend weeks understanding how a family actually moves through a day: where the morning light should fall, where the dog needs a door.

02

Crafted by experts.

Our stoneworkers, framers, and finish carpenters have worked together for decades. We don't rotate crews; we build trust that shows in the joinery.

03

Built with precision.

Every load calculation, every reveal, every mitered corner is checked twice. Precision isn't a finishing touch here, it's the whole method.

04

A Big Sky home is built to outlast trends and be handed down. We're still fielding calls about homes we finished twenty years ago.

The Robert Family

"We never set out to build a company. We set out to build homes we'd be proud to sign."
